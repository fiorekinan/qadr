A space to keep your Ramadan Dua.

Qadr is a dedicated page that presents essential supplications for Suhoor and Iftar in a clear and accessible format. It is designed to help users revisit and reflect on the daily prayers of Ramadan with ease.

Sahoor Dua

بِرَحْمَةِ اللَّهِ الْمُسْتَغْفِرِينَ

"May Allah send His mercy upon those who partake in suhoor."

Iftar Dua

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

"The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is assured, if Allah wills."
